7 Days – 5 Nights

Classic Armenia

7 days5 nights13 sights
Duration: 7 Days / 5 Nights

Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Yerevan

Meeting at Zvartnots International Airport and transfer to your hotel in Yerevan. Check in and rest after the flight.

Day 2

Erebuni, Vernissage & Matenadaran

Visit the Erebuni Fortress and museum, where Yerevan was founded in 782 BC, stroll through the Vernissage open-air market, see the Matenadaran and take a tour of the city.

Dinner is optional, on request, and is not included in the tour price.

Day 3

Zvartnots, Etchmiadzin & Noah’s Harbor

See the ruins of Zvartnots Cathedral, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, visit Etchmiadzin Cathedral and the Noah’s Harbor cultural park.

Day 4

Areni & Jermuk

Visit a winery in Areni with a wine tasting, then take a tour of Jermuk, the mountain spa town famous for its mineral water.

Dinner is not included in the tour price.

Day 5

Garni, Symphony of Stones & Geghard

Visit the pagan temple of Garni, admire the basalt columns of the Symphony of Stones, explore the Geghard Monastery complex and watch a traditional lavash-baking ceremony.

Day 6

Dargett & Sevanavank

Visit the Dargett craft brewery and Sevanavank Monastery on the peninsula of Lake Sevan.

Dinner is optional, on request, and is not included in the tour price.

Day 7

Departure

Check out from the hotel and transfer to Zvartnots International Airport. Have a safe journey home!
