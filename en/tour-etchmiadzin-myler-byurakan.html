Etchmiadzin, MyLer & Byurakan

5 days3 nights6 sights
Duration: 5 Days / 3 Nights

Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Yerevan

Meeting at Zvartnots International Airport and transfer to your hotel in Yerevan. Check in and rest after the flight.

Day 2

Etchmiadzin & Machanents

Visit Etchmiadzin Cathedral, the spiritual centre of the Armenian Church, take a tour of Machanents house, and enjoy an evening walk around Yerevan.

Dinner with Armenian dishes is optional, on request, and is not included in the tour price.

Day 3

MyLer Mountain

A day of skiing and snowboarding at the MyLer Mountain resort, with views of Mount Aragats.

Skiing depends on snow and weather conditions.

Day 4

Voskevaz, Byurakan & Ohanavank

Visit the Voskevaz winery, the Byurakan Astrophysical Observatory and the medieval Ohanavank Monastery on the edge of the Kasakh gorge.

A feast afterwards is optional, on request, and is not included in the tour price.

Day 5

Departure

Check out from the hotel and transfer to Zvartnots International Airport. Have a safe journey home!
