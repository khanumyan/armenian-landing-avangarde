Yerevan, Gyumri & Dilijan

6 days5 nights8 sights
Duration: 6 Days / 5 Nights

Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival & Welcome to Yerevan

Meeting at Zvartnots International Airport and transfer to your hotel in Yerevan. Check in and rest after the flight.

Day 2

Matenadaran & Yerevan

Visit the Matenadaran, the world-famous repository of ancient Armenian manuscripts, tour the Yerevan Brandy Factory with a tasting of Armenian brandy, and enjoy a walk around the city centre.

Dinner is optional, on request, and is not included in the tour price.

Day 3

Byurakan Observatory & Gyumri

Visit the Byurakan Astrophysical Observatory on the slopes of Mount Aragats, then continue to Gyumri, Armenia’s second city, for a tour of its historic Kumayri district and 19th-century architecture.

Dinner is optional, on request, and is not included in the tour price.

Day 4

MyLer Mountain

A day of winter fun at the MyLer Mountain ski resort: skiing, snowboarding and fresh mountain air with views of Mount Aragats.

Ski activities depend on snow and weather conditions.

Day 5

Dilijan, Ijevan & Lake Sevan

Discover green Dilijan, the “Armenian Switzerland”, visit a winery in Ijevan with a tasting of local wines, and stop at Lake Sevan for dinner on the way back.

Dinner at Lake Sevan is not included in the tour price.

Day 6

Departure

Check out from the hotel and transfer to Zvartnots International Airport. Have a safe journey home!
